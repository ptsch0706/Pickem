Set the roster

Enter the four players. Draft order follows the list top to bottom. It's a snake draft: round 1 goes 1→2→3→4, round 2 flips to 4→3→2→1, round 3 back to 1→2→3→4, and so on.

Use the arrows to reorder players, or randomize for a fair draw. Order reverses each round (snake draft) until all 32 teams are gone — 8 rounds each.
